Status: ACTIVE

Canonical job:

`Есть ли риск, что я сейчас трачу рекламу на товары без доступного остатка? Возьми активную CPC-кампанию с максимальным расходом за 29 августа — 4 сентября 2026 года из уже проверенной Performance-статистики, сам получи список рекламируемых товаров и сверь их с текущими остатками Ozon. Покажи товары без остатка отдельно от товаров с положительным остатком; отсутствие строки не считай нулём и не проси меня вручную перечислять SKU.`

Capability target: prove the worker can preserve one business job across sequential reads from two materially different Bridge/provider surfaces — Performance advertising campaign contents and Seller current stock — and correlate them without operator-supplied SKU lists.

Selection authority from completed CAP-18:
- highest displayed spend among active CPC campaigns for `2026-08-29`..`2026-09-04`: campaign `37130634`, `Печать Реком 21,03 27.08.2026`;
- period spend: `3,871.74 RUB`;
- attributed order value: `8,500 RUB`;
- DRR: `45.5%`.

Active registry authority:
- `performance_campaign_products` -> `GET /api/client/campaign/{campaignId}/v2/products`, READ_SAFE/current, explicit pagination, purpose: obtain campaign product list.
- `stocks_current` -> `POST /v4/product/info/stocks`, READ_SAFE/current, purpose: current aggregate stock.

Frozen method:
1. Read `performance_campaign_products` for campaign `37130634`, page `1`, pageSize `100`.
2. If the provider proves another page exists, continue only through separate explicit page commands; no hidden autopagination.
3. Extract the actual provider-returned product/SKU identifiers. Do not ask the operator to enumerate them and do not substitute catalog history for the live campaign membership read.
4. After campaign membership is complete, call `stocks_current` for exactly those returned product identifiers using the supported `filter.product_id` surface and a sufficient explicit limit.
5. Separate positive stock, explicit zero stock, and provider omission. Missing rows are `UNKNOWN/OMITTED`, never silently converted to zero.
6. This test is advertising × stock correlation. Do not use Seller Analytics, campaign spend, visibility, or historical sales as a substitute for current stock.
7. One logical command -> at most one physical business request per run.

Scoring: standard CAP fields, with the key proof being `multi_run_orchestration` across Performance + Seller stock surfaces.
