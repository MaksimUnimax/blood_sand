## 2. What the market already does well

### Marketplaces

Ozon/Wildberries/Yandex Market are structurally stronger at:
- familiar checkout;
- availability/delivery expectations;
- ratings/reviews/UGC;
- product alternatives;
- platform trust/returns;
- structured price/product fields;
- large internal shopping demand.

Blood & Sand should use those strengths where useful, not try to recreate their network effects as the main website advantage.

### Specialist commerce

Fresh Stage-07 comparison proves that the following are **not unique advantages**:
- broad symbolic assortment;
- exact material/size/weight alone;
- direct own-site checkout;
- hybrid direct + WB/Ozon/Yandex Market checkout;
- reviews existing;
- a generic meaning article;
- having an auto-obereg category;
- explaining at least some mounting options.

Competitors such as Veles.bz, Ярило, Simvolroda, BEREGY, Ruyan, oberegi-runi, Slavyarmarka and HappyWitch already cover meaningful parts of those jobs.

## 3. Accepted defensible Blood & Sand capabilities

A competitive advantage is accepted only where all three exist: user need + competitor gap + Blood & Sand capability/evidence asset.

### A. REAL_CAR_PRODUCT_EVIDENCE

**SUPPORTED / DEFENSIBLE CAPABILITY**

Need:
- Stage-06 buyer/category evidence repeatedly flags appearance in car, scale, attachment, cord/material and heat/sun concerns.

Gap:
- specialist competitors often publish dimensions/materials, but the bounded comparison did not find a systematic standard for exact SKU in a real cabin, driver-view scale, hanging length and repeatable mounting-construction proof.

Capability:
- Blood & Sand controls the actual products and can create exact-SKU photos/video/tests.

Required implementation evidence later:
- exact product in real car;
- driver-view and windshield-view scale;
- total hanging length;
- mirror attachment and adjustment path;
- component close-ups;
- honest limitations/fit.

### B. EXACT_MOUNTING_CONSTRUCTION

**SUPPORTED / DEFENSIBLE WHEN MATERIALIZED**

Not just “крепится на зеркало”. The site should document:
- exact loop/cord construction;
- adjustment/removal method;
- bead/tassel relation;
- fit constraints;
- what is measured vs seller-declared vs unknown.

This directly answers a buyer problem that current marketplace copy and specialist samples solve inconsistently.

### C. SOURCE_RIGOR

**SUPPORTED / DEFENSIBLE CAPABILITY — STRONG**

Stage 07.3 confirms a real source-quality gap.

Strongest case: Vegvísir.
A current specialist page describes it as an ancient Scandinavian/Viking navigation symbol and places galdrastafir in IV–VII centuries. The checked manuscript/scholarly chain instead places the earliest known Vegvísir attestation in the 1860 Huld manuscript and current peer-reviewed scholarship treats galdrastafir within later Icelandic/European grimoire traditions rather than as straightforward Viking-Age rune signs.

The same method reveals claim-boundary problems around:
- modern Pechat Velesa graphics vs historically attested Veles/Volos;
- Alatyr folklore stone vs modern graphic emblem;
- medieval `ægishjálmr` word/concept vs later drawn Icelandic stave.

Blood & Sand source hierarchy:
1. primary/manuscript/archive/recorded folklore;
2. peer-reviewed/academic;
3. institutional/reference encyclopedia;
4. commercial/popular/esoteric only for attributed modern belief/usage.

Future content must distinguish:
- `ATTESTED`;
- `SCHOLARLY_INTERPRETATION`;
- `MODERN_INTERPRETATION`;
- `SELLER_OR_PRACTITIONER_BELIEF`;
- `UNSUPPORTED_EFFECT_CLAIM`.

### D. EVIDENCE_BASED_SELECTION

**SUPPORTED / DEFENSIBLE IF BUILT ON OBSERVABLE DIFFERENCES**

Competitors already publish chooser content. The Blood & Sand version only becomes better if it helps the user choose by:
- actual product form/size/material;
- real-car placement and visibility;
- current product family differences;
- sourced historical distinction;
- explicit modern interpretation where relevant;
- product availability and commerce path.

It must not use magical efficacy as an objective selection variable.

### E. KNOWLEDGE_TO_EXACT_SKU

**SUPPORTED / HIGH BAR**

Generic blog→catalog linking is parity. The defensible version is:
`sourced explanation → explicit choice rule → exact current SKU → exact physical/use evidence → purchase path`.
