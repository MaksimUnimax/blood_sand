# 1. OU02 Печать Велеса vs OU06 broader Veles family

Decision: **KEEP BOTH as distinct opportunity/job lanes; model as provisional parent/child relationship, not duplicates.**

## OU02 — Печать Велеса

Direct user job:
- understand/evaluate a specific named symbol/form (`Печать Велеса`, bear/wolf paw);
- compare meaning/suitability;
- evaluate/buy the specific symbol product.

Evidence:
- dedicated high demand for `печать велеса`;
- base Search strongly product/transactional;
- dedicated `печать велеса значение` meaning-first Search job;
- bear/wolf variants have their own observed demand;
- specialist product pages rank inside both commercial and meaning contexts.

## OU06 — broader Veles family

Direct user job:
- understand what counts as a Veles amulet/symbol family;
- choose among forms associated with Veles;
- decide which form fits the user's goal.

Evidence:
- `оберег велес` itself has meaningful demand;
- Alice explicitly says Veles amulet is not one universal symbol but a group and enumerates several forms;
- Alice gives task-based choice guidance and multiple products.

## Provisional relationship

`Veles family chooser / overview` → `specific named forms such as Печать Велеса / bear-wolf variants`

This is a **semantic/job hierarchy**, not a requirement for parent/child URLs.

Stage 06 must answer:

- whether ассортимент реально supports a broad family chooser;
- whether users/buyers choose first by `Велес` family or by exact visual symbol.

# 2. OU03 Automotive protection vs OU04 Mirror-pendant form factor

Decision: **KEEP SEPARATE. Distinct jobs are directly proven.**

## OU03 — automotive protection

User job:
- choose a protective/symbolic item for car/driver;
- understand protection/use/suitability;
- buy an appropriate car amulet.

Evidence:
- Search mixed commercial + choice/use-case;
- Alice provides selection/use guidance + direct products;
- `амулет в машину` collapses into this same semantic protection cluster;
- strong mobile-first demand.

## OU04 — mirror-pendant form factor

User job:
- choose/buy something to hang on the rear-view mirror;
- evaluate form/design/material/personalization/decor.

Evidence:
- near-pure transactional Search;
- Alice broadens to decor/fandom/personalization as well as symbolic products;
- form factor is wider than protection intent.

## Why one product does not merge the opportunities

A Blood & Sand mirror pendant can satisfy **both** jobs, but the user's starting problem differs:
- `оберег в машину` starts from **function/meaning**;
- `подвеска на зеркало в машину` starts from **object/form factor**.

Therefore the opportunities stay separate through research and commercial-model stages.

Stage 06 must answer:
- which SKUs are genuinely designed for mirror hanging;
- which are sold/bought primarily as protection symbols vs decor/accessory;
- whether the same SKU needs different messaging/entry paths.

Stage 07 must answer:
- whether specialist differentiation can overcome OU04's marketplace/commodity pressure.
