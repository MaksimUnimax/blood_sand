# R2 — Secondary Search final review — 2026-08-26

Status: **COMPLETE — paid secondary expansion STOPPED**

This document closes the evidence-driven secondary Search expansion for Roadmap 04. It does not assign final Page Jobs or information architecture; those decisions belong to Roadmap 05.

## Measurements completed

### A1 — `оберег по знаку зодиака`

Result: **lexical narrowing did not create a clean symbolic/Slavic lane.**

The returned Top-10 remained a mixture of zodiac-selection/editorial, stones/jewelry and marketplaces. No Slavic-symbol specialist site appeared in the returned Top-10.

Interpretation: changing wording toward `оберег` is not enough to create a separate job when the underlying human intent remains broad zodiac selection.

### A2 — `печать велеса значение`

Result: **distinct meaning-first Search job confirmed.**

Compared with the transactional/product-heavy base `печать велеса`, the explicit `значение` modifier shifted the Top-10 toward explanation/meaning. Specialist commerce remained competitive: `slavyanskieoberegi.ru` occupied #1, #7, #9 and #10, while generic marketplaces disappeared from the returned Top-10.

Interpretation: for named symbols, explanatory demand can form a separate Search job while still supporting commercial specialist pages.

### A3 — `амулет в машину`

Result: **no separate job.**

Yandex largely folded the query back into the `оберег в машину` semantic cluster. Most titles/URLs used `оберег`; marketplace commerce was strong, but explanatory/protection branches remained.

Interpretation: `амулет` is a lexical/supporting variant of the automotive-protection cluster, not evidence for a separate provisional Page Job.

### B1 — `вегвизир значение`

Result: **distinct meaning-first Search job confirmed in a second symbol family.**

The Top-10 became overwhelmingly history/origin/meaning/explanation-led, with no Ozon, Wildberries or Yandex Market. This independently reproduces the modifier split observed for `печать велеса значение`.

Interpretation: the named-symbol `значение` layer is repeatable across symbol families rather than a one-query anomaly.

### B2 — `шлем ужаса оберег`

Result: **coherent named-symbol commercial lane confirmed.**

Direct Top-10 composition:
- Wildberries #1;
- Ozon #2;
- `slavyanskieoberegi.ru` #3;
- Yandex Market #4/#7/#8/#10;
- Livemaster catalog #5;
- Livemaster explanatory article #6;
- `swargas.ru` historical/specialist content #9.

The result set stays tightly centered on `Шлем Ужаса` / `Агисхьяльм`, protection, amulet/obereg products and meaning/history. It is not diffused into unrelated Norse topics.

Interpretation: `Шлем Ужаса` is a real adjacent named-symbol opportunity with strong commercial intent and a supporting explanatory layer. It should be evaluated in Roadmap 05 as its own named-symbol opportunity, not dismissed as merely a Vegvisir comparison term.

## Cross-secondary conclusions

### 1. Meaning intent is structurally real for named symbols

Confirmed independently twice:
- `печать велеса` → transactional/product, while `печать велеса значение` → meaning-first;
- `вегвизир` → mixed entity + commercial, while `вегвизир значение` → meaning/history-first.

This is strong evidence that named-symbol demand has at least two recurring user jobs: learn/understand and evaluate/buy. Whether these become separate pages or one hybrid architecture is deliberately deferred to Roadmap 05.

### 2. Named adjacent symbols can be commercially coherent

`шлем ужаса оберег` shows a tight commerce-first lane with both marketplaces and specialist symbolic content. This strengthens the broader named-symbol opportunity model beyond only the already-measured Vegvisir and Slavic symbols.

### 3. Lexical variants should not be promoted to jobs without evidence

`амулет в машину` collapses into the `оберег в машину` cluster. The vocabulary belongs in semantic coverage, not automatically in separate IA.

### 4. Narrowing a contaminated root does not guarantee clean intent

`оберег по знаку зодиака` still inherits stones/jewelry/zodiac-selection contamination. The zodiac opportunity must be treated carefully and should not be modeled from broad volume alone.

## Paid secondary stop decision

**STOP further paid secondary Search expansion now.**

Reasons:
- meaning-layer behavior is already replicated across two independent symbol families;
- the remaining adjacent named-symbol structural hypothesis was answered by B2;
- additional `значение` variants have low marginal decision value;
- automotive synonym expansion already showed lexical collapse;
- gift and generic decor branches were already shown to have weak or commodity product fit.

Further paid Search requests require a new explicit decision question generated by later analysis, not curiosity or wording variation.

## Evidence inputs carried forward to Roadmap 05

Provisional only:
- named-symbol opportunities should be modeled with both explanatory and commercial jobs in mind;
- specialist content-commerce pages can rank in both layers;
- `Шлем Ужаса` is a credible adjacent named-symbol lane;
- automotive `амулет` is semantic coverage inside the `оберег в машину` cluster;
- zodiac roots remain contaminated and require narrower opportunity treatment.
