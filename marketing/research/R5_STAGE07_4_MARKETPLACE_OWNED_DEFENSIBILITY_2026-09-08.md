## 1. What marketplaces already solve better

For the current project, Ozon/WB/Yandex Market should be treated as strong infrastructure/trust competitors, not as content templates to copy.

They are structurally strong at:
- familiar checkout;
- saved accounts/payment methods;
- availability and delivery expectations;
- ratings/reviews/UGC;
- product discovery and alternatives;
- platform trust/returns;
- large internal shopping demand;
- structured price/product attributes.

R2 measured marketplace presence directly across most commercial opportunity roots. Stage 06 additionally proves Blood & Sand itself already operates deep current assortments on Ozon/WB.

Therefore an owned site should **not** spend its main differentiation budget trying to recreate marketplace network effects.

Likely commercial design implication for later Stage 09:
- direct checkout may be useful;
- marketplace exits may be useful;
- neither is the content moat.

## 2. What specialist competitors already solve well

07.2 eliminates several supposed advantages:
- catalogue breadth;
- exact dimensions/materials alone;
- direct checkout;
- hybrid marketplace checkout;
- reviews existing;
- basic meaning articles;
- automotive-category existence;
- basic attachment descriptions.

These become parity requirements or optional commercial infrastructure.

## 3. Combined owned-site advantage stack

The defensible opportunity is a **combination**, not one feature:

### A. Exact product evidence in real use
- exact SKU in a real vehicle;
- hanging length and scale;
- driver-view / windshield visibility;
- component close-ups;
- mirror attachment/cord construction;
- honest limits and fit.

User-need basis:
Stage-06 analog evidence repeatedly identifies appearance-in-car, size, cord/attachment, material and heat/sun concerns.

Competitor-gap basis:
07.2 found many exact dimensions/materials, but no systematic real-car engineering/use standard across the bounded specialist sample.

Capability basis:
Blood & Sand owns/controls the actual product and can create exact-SKU evidence.

### B. Source-rigorous meaning/history
- direct source citations when possible;
- scholarly interpretation separated from primary evidence;
- modern/esoteric interpretation labelled as such;
- no unsupported efficacy claim stated as fact.

User/Search basis:
R2 proved separate meaning/history jobs for named symbols.

Competitor gap:
07.3 found repeated claim-boundary problems and a direct Vegvísir chronology contradiction in current specialist content.

Capability:
A concrete source hierarchy and claim-label standard is now defined.

### C. Evidence-based selection → exact SKU
- compare actual current products by observable differences;
- preserve symbol-family boundaries;
- connect sourced meaning to exact product facts;
- let beliefs remain beliefs rather than turning them into selection facts.

This is stronger than a generic blog-to-product link.
