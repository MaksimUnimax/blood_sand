## 1. What competitors already do well

### 1.1 Deep assortment is already common

`veles.bz/catalog/oberegi-dlya-avtomobilya` currently exposes **37 automotive products**. The same category includes multiple Pechat/Veles items, a separate Veles item, Alatyr, Vegvisir and other symbols. This is a direct competitor to the notion that Blood & Sand can win simply by having many symbolic automotive SKUs.

`simvolroda.ru` maintains deep Veles and wider Slavic taxonomies with multiple materials, product forms and symbol families. `slavyanskieoberegi.ru` likewise has a deep Veles/Pechat product family and associated knowledge content.

**Decision:** `ASSORTMENT_BREADTH` is a parity requirement, not a defensible advantage.

### 1.2 Exact dimensions/materials are already a strong specialist norm

Examples observed in the current pass:

- Ярило Pechat sample: silver/bronze variants, about 10 g, `30×17 mm`, direct cart, production lead time and gift box.
- A second current Ярило Pechat sample: `32 mm`, about `22 g`, silver 925, two-sided construction, blackening and explicit gift-box packaging.
- Simvolroda Veles product: silver 925 with blackening, `30 mm`, about `14.5 g`, direct cart/one-click and delivery/payment details.
- oberegi-runi Pechat: silver 925, `38 mm` and `46 mm`, blackening; related leather cords expose `450/600 mm` length and `3 mm` diameter.
- BEREGY Pechat: bronze/melchior, about `5 g`, `32×22×5 mm`, exact `2 mm` cord/chain hole, blackening and casting technology.
- Veles.bz individual products commonly expose material, size/diameter, weight, lead time and item reviews.

**Decision:** `MATERIAL + SIZE + WEIGHT` by itself is not differentiation. Blood & Sand must match this minimum but cannot present it as the core advantage.

### 1.3 Hybrid direct + marketplace checkout is already implemented

A fresher BEREGY current-page recheck directly exposes:
- `Купить на Wildberries`;
- `Купить на Ozon`;
- `Купить на Яндекс.Маркет`;
- direct cart/buy-now on the own page.

This corrects the narrower 07.1 sample that failed to expose those strings in the captured page text.

**Decision:** hybrid checkout remains strategically useful, but it is **not unique**. It is a parity/UX pattern, not the differentiator.

### 1.4 Specialist social proof can be substantial

Ярило's current sampled Pechat page exposes **17 reviews** and customer review photos. BEREGY exposes product ratings/reviews. Veles.bz exposes product-level review counts and individual reviews on sampled products.

**Decision:** an owned site cannot assume specialist competitors are weak on trust. Blood & Sand should plan its trust layer as a requirement, not as an automatic advantage.

### 1.5 Knowledge↔commerce linkage is already present

Several specialists already connect explanatory content to products:
- Ярило: Veles/Pechat family pages, product copy, related products and selection/FAQ services;
- Simvolroda: symbol-meaning index with `Изделия` paths plus product taxonomy;
- Ruyan: symbol meaning directly inside commerce pages;
- Veles.bz: portal/category/explanatory copy integrated with large catalogs;
- HappyWitch: automotive choice article returning the user to the store/consultants.

**Decision:** merely publishing a blog beside a catalog is not enough. Blood & Sand must make the knowledge layer materially more useful or more trustworthy.

## 2. Automotive product-experience comparison

### 2.1 `veles.bz` — strongest current direct auto-specialist benchmark

Observed current auto category:
- 37 products;
- multiple symbolic families;
- direct prices and product links;
- explicit copy about choosing by purpose, material and convenience of use/mounting;
- product pages with material/size/lead-time facts.

What was **not observed as a systematic standard** in the sampled current category/product evidence:
- repeatable driver-view / windshield-view photos showing actual obstruction/scale;
- exact hanging-loop/cord path and adjustment construction across products;
- a standardized `how it hangs on the mirror` block;
- measured swing/clearance/visibility guidance.

This does not prove those assets are absent from every product. It proves they were not observed as a repeated category standard in the bounded sample.

### 2.2 `slavyarmarka.ru` — important mounting benchmark

The available public category evidence explicitly distinguishes:
- mirror-hanging products;
- at least one panel-mounted product using double-sided tape;
- item materials, handwork and some dimensions;
- multiple automotive product types.

Therefore Blood & Sand cannot claim `we explain where it attaches` as unique.

However, the available crawl is older than the core 07.2 samples and did not show a systematic real-car scale/driver-visibility documentation standard. This competitor remains a **mounting benchmark**, not final freshness authority.

### 2.3 `happywitch.ru` — choice-guide benchmark

The automotive guide demonstrates that informational `how to choose an obereg for a car` content can compete and feed a store. It covers multiple traditions/forms and gives a consultation path.

But the guide is primarily belief/use-case based. It does not solve the same engineering/product-documentation job as:
- actual pendant scale;
- exact mirror attachment construction;
- materials under heat/sun exposure;
- visibility/obstruction in a real cabin;
- exact package and physical configuration.

**Decision:** Blood & Sand's best auto-content opportunity is not another generic `which talisman protects the driver` article. It is a stronger **real-product use and selection layer**.

## 3. Candidate advantage status after 07.2

### H1 — real product documentation

Status: **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED / NARROWED**.

Unsupported broad version:
> We can win because competitors do not provide exact product facts.

This is false. Many do.

Still-supported narrow version:
> Blood & Sand may differentiate by documenting the actual automotive use of its exact pendant better than current specialist samples: scale in a real cabin, driver-view visibility, mirror mounting/cord construction, hanging length, component close-ups and honest physical limitations.

Why this remains plausible:
- Stage-06 buyer/category evidence specifically identifies scale, attachment, cord, material/aging and appearance-in-car as buyer concerns;
- current specialist competitors often provide generic dimensions/materials but did not show a systematic real-car engineering standard in the bounded 07.2 sample;
- Blood & Sand directly controls the physical product and can create those assets.

### H3 — knowledge → selection → exact product

Status: **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED / HIGH BAR**.

Competitors already link knowledge to products. Therefore the defensible version must be stronger:
- explicit choice criteria;
- compare symbols/products by user task without fabricating efficacy;
- explain differences between same-family products;
- tie explanations to exact physical SKUs;
- show real-use constraints and mounting;
- preserve source provenance for historical claims.

### H4 — hybrid checkout

Status: **USEFUL BUT NOT DEFENSIBLE**.

BEREGY is direct counterevidence to uniqueness. Keep hybrid checkout as a likely commercial-model option for Stage 09, but do not score it as a competitive moat.

### H5 — OU04 mirror-pendant form factor

Status after 07.2: **CONDITIONAL**.

Product fit is strong, but specialist and marketplace competitors already sell and explain hanging products. The only still-plausible owned advantage is superior actual-use documentation/selection, not the form factor itself.

## 6. Still-live candidate advantages

1. **REAL_CAR_PRODUCT_EVIDENCE** — exact SKU in real cabin; scale, visibility, hanging length, component/mounting close-ups.
2. **EXACT_MOUNTING_CONSTRUCTION** — how the specific cord/loop/beads/tassel attach and adjust; what can/cannot fit.
3. **SOURCE_RIGOR** — historical/ethnographic claims separated from modern reconstruction and seller belief; must be tested in 07.3.
4. **EVIDENCE_BASED_SELECTION** — choose by observable task/product properties while clearly labelling belief/interpretation.
5. **KNOWLEDGE_TO_EXACT_SKU** — explanation and selection connected to current products without turning unsupported claims into product facts.

These are not yet all final accepted advantages. 07.3 must prove or reject `SOURCE_RIGOR`, then 07.4 tests owned-site defensibility against marketplaces and reopened zodiac.
