### `Несколько советов`

Alice advises:
- choose a symbol personally meaningful to the user;
- choose by goal: theft, accidents, calmness;
- avoid heavy/sharp hanging objects for safety;
- religious symbols may be blessed in a church;
- periodically "cleanse" the amulet;
- avoid explicitly negative imagery.

## Evidence-safe interpretation

1. **Consumer Alice treats `оберег в машину` as a recommendation / selection problem, not just an informational definition.**
   It first explains categories, then gives decision criteria, then presents purchasable examples.

2. **The answer is explicitly multi-tradition.**
   Slavic symbolism is only one branch alongside Christian, feng-shui/other cultural and generic/practical talismans.

3. **Automotive hanging form is directly recognized.**
   Alice explicitly mentions wood pendants hung on the rear-view mirror, and one surfaced product is named `Подвеска в машину на зеркало ... "Колесо Перуна"`.

4. **Slavic symbols receive strong placement inside the answer.**
   `Путник`, `коловрат`, and `Колесо Перуна` appear in the first category, and two of the first product examples are Slavic hanging products.

5. **Alice itself bridges informational intent into commerce.**
   The answer contains explanatory content + a product recommendation layer with live merchant/price cards. This is materially different from a pure informational answer.

6. **This creates direct GEO/AEO opportunity for Blood & Sand.**
